| **Fed / inflation** | Fed (Chair Warsh) **hiked** to 3.75–4.00% in Sept, first hike since 2023; Sept CPI 3.7% y/y (+0.6% m/m), core 2.6%; ~54% odds of another hike Oct 27–28. | Rate-shock regime like 1994/2022: stock–bond correlation positive (+0.42 in 2026). Bonds hedge less. |
| **War / oil** | US–Iran war, Strait of Hormuz disruptions, Houthi attacks on Aramco, Russia–Ukraine; Brent ≈ $105 (+70% YTD), Dec-2027 futures ~$86 (curve priced for prolonged disruption). | Energy & commodities are the live inflation hedge (XLE +52%, GSG +58% YTD). |
| **Dollar** | DXY ≈ 102, 18-month high after hot CPI; +3.4% y/y. | UUP is working (+7% YTD) as a risk-off/hawkish-Fed hedge. |
| **Gold** | ≈ $4,170, −27% from Jan high $5,608; fell 8% in Sept as 10y > 5%; central banks still buying (Sept ~63t, PBoC 21t). | Hurt by high real yields; still a war/debasement hedge, now cheaper. |
| **Credit** | HY OAS ~3.1–3.25% (tight) but CCC ~1,050bp (multi-year wide); private-credit defaults ~5.8%. | Avoid junk / high-yield; stress is building at the weak end. |
| **Growth** | Recession odds ~20% (12m); GDP ~1.8–2.2%; unemployment drifting up. | Not the base case, but a tech-led selloff could tip it — keep deflation hedge (intermediate Treasuries). |
| **Managed futures** | KMLM +13% by May, DBMF +3.4% in Sept; trend has been long commodities / short bonds. | Core hedge is doing its job. |

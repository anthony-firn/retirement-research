Research library

About the research

This project studies how people can prepare for retirement and turn savings into dependable spending. Its aim is an open, reproducible decision resource, with useful rejected ideas as well as successful ones.

Start with the research history, how to reproduce and extend a search, data provenance, and the experiment record template.

What the evidence means

A simulated paycheck is a model output, not promised income. Many fund histories are reconstructions from periods before the funds existed. Reshuffling blocks gives different sequences of the same observations, not independent new market evidence. Taxes and benefit rules are simplified. The application currently emphasizes US retirement planning and California tax examples.

The application questionnaire scores a limited set of operable strategies. It does not discover the optimal retirement plan for every person, nor does it search the entire research frontier at runtime. The original Atlas has a broader comparison set.

What belongs here

  • A question, hypothesis, baseline, dated sources and explicit assumptions.
  • Commands, seeds, code/data fingerprints, full risk and income results, and a verdict.
  • Failed candidates, sensitivity tests, limitations and evidence that would change the conclusion.
  • Accessible explanations linked to the underlying experiment, rather than a leaderboard ranked by return alone.

Household facts, original assistant transcripts, account details, private files, credentials and broker statements do not belong in a public library. Translate private conversations into reviewed, anonymous research questions. Existing tracked files still need a publication review; being in Git does not establish redistribution rights or remove privacy concerns.

Ways to contribute

You can improve plain-language explanations, reproduce one result, challenge a proxy, add a country-specific assumptions module, test accessibility with older adults, or propose a cheaper and simpler implementation. A well-explained failure is useful. See CONTRIBUTING.md.
